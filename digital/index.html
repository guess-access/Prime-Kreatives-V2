What exactly do I receive?

Editable source files in the tool listed on the pack, a PDF guide, and a short video walkthrough. Nothing is locked to my account — you can duplicate and edit everything.

Can I use these for client work?

Yes. One licence covers unlimited use inside your own business and for your own clients. You cannot resell the templates themselves as a product.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The reporting pack assumes you can fill in a spreadsheet. The SOP kit is written and formatted — you fill in the blanks. If you get stuck, email support.

What if I want the finished thing instead?

That is the done-for-you service. The price of your pack comes off the quote, so you do not pay twice.

What currency will I be invoiced in?

USD by default. AUD and PHP are available — just say which on the order form.
